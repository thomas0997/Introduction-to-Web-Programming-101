Yoga

Build flexibility, balance, and calm with guided flows suited to every experience level. Classes run 45 minutes and all mats and props are provided.

Tip: Arrive 10 minutes early to reserve a mat near the front for the best view of the instructor.

Strength

A full-body circuit using free weights and resistance machines to build muscle and improve bone density. Modifications are offered for every fitness level.

Tip: Bring a water bottle and a towel; this class moves quickly between stations.

Cardio

High-energy interval training designed to raise your heart rate, burn calories, and boost endurance. No equipment experience required.

Tip: Wear supportive athletic shoes; this class includes jumping intervals.
