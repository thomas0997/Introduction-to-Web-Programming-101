Fuel Your Body Right

Food for Thought

Mindset

Good nutrition is a lifestyle, not a diet. It starts with a new way of thinking about food. Think of your body as a vehicle that uses food for fuel. Fill your tank with the right type of fuel.

What to Eat

Quick Guide

Use the following as a guideline:

  • Protein, such as eggs, chicken, and fish
  • Vegetables, but avoid canned
  • Fruit
  • Fat, such as avocado and olive oil

Meal of the Week

30 Min • Serves 4

Herb Roasted Chicken

Ingredients:

  • Boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Fresh rosemary and thyme

Season the chicken and roast at 400 degrees F until cooked through, about 20-25 minutes. Let rest for a few minutes before serving.
